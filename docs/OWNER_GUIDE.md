# How to use Muscle House Gym OS every day

**You don't need to remember anything. The system remembers and tells you.**

## Every morning (2 minutes)
1. At 8:00 AM you get a **WhatsApp briefing**: today's follow-ups, renewals due, unpaid invoices, birthdays and the 5 people to contact first.
2. Tap the link in it, or open the app — you land on **Today**.

## The Today screen — work top to bottom
Cards are grouped **Act now → Today → This week → When you have time**. Every card already shows what you need to know:
- what they're interested in and their concerns (e.g. *Personal training*, *Concern: Price*)
- any **promise** you made ("10% off if he joins before 30 Nov")
- the **last conversation** and who had it

For each card:
| Tap | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Call** | Phones them directly |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens a chat with a ready-written message |
| **Done** | Clears it from today |
| **Snooze** | Brings it back tomorrow / in 3 days / next week |
| **Open** | Opens their full profile |

## After every conversation (15 seconds)
Open the customer → **Log follow-up** → tap what happened (*Interested*, *Call back later*, *No answer*…) → tap their interests or concerns → **Save**.
Typing is optional — tap **Speak note** to dictate. The next follow-up date is set for you.

If you promise something, add it under **Memory → Promises made**. If you need a nudge, tap **Remind me** and pick *Tomorrow / In 3 days / Next week*.

## When someone walks in
Tap the search bar (or press `/`) and type the first digits of their mobile number. You instantly see their status, interests, promises and the last conversation — and can **Convert to membership** from there.

## What happens automatically
- Thank-you message when an enquiry is saved (if they agreed to WhatsApp)
- Follow-up messages on the follow-up date, expiry reminders 7/3/1/0 days before, renewal follow-up 3 days after expiry, a "we miss you" message after 14 days
- Birthday and membership-anniversary wishes
- Membership confirmations, invoices, pause confirmations
- Members flagged **Likely to drop** (late renewals, long pauses, unpaid balance) and **upgrade suggestions** (e.g. "Renewed Monthly 3 times — Quarterly saves ₹500")

## Settings you may want to change
**Settings → Reminders**: your WhatsApp number, morning briefing time, optional evening summary, and "Send me a test briefing".
**Settings → Interest & objection tags**: add tags like *Zumba* or *Parking*.
**Automations**: switch any automatic message on or off and see every message that was sent, simulated or failed.
